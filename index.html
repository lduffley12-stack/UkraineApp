How to get a free Azure Speech key (5 min)
  1. Go to portal.azure.com and sign in (create a free account if needed — a credit card is required but you won't be charged on the free tier).
  2. In the top search bar type Speech services and click the result.
  3. Click Create. Fill in:
    • Subscription: your free one
    • Resource group: create a new one, any name
    • Region: pick one close to you (e.g. East US) — remember this, you'll enter it here
    • Name: anything (e.g. my-ukrainian-app)
    • Pricing tier: Free F0 (500k characters/month)
  4. Click Review + create, then Create. Wait ~30 seconds for deployment.
  5. Click Go to resource → in the left sidebar, click Keys and Endpoint.
  6. Copy KEY 1 and paste it above. Pick the same Region you chose in step 3.
  7. Click Test voice. You should hear "Привіт".

Your key is stored only in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves this machine.
